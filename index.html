Services

Three ways to work together

Every service I offer uses the P.E.A.R.L Framework: Product, Engagement, Activation, Retention and Leverage. It helps me pinpoint where your product is leaking value, slowing growth or failing the people it was built for, so you know what to fix first.

PEARL Readiness Review
£1,999

A focused product diagnosis. In one review, you get a clear, independent view of where your product is falling short and what to fix first.

Most teams are too close to their own product to judge it clearly. Founders, developers, and agency partners all have a stake in the outcome, which makes it harder to spot where the product is underperforming, where trust is being lost, or where users are quietly dropping away. This review gives you clarity fast, before more time and money go into the wrong area.

What's included
  • 45 minute prep call with founder, CTO, or product lead
  • Hands-on PEARL diagnostic across Product, Engagement, Activation, Retention, and Leverage
  • Focused stakeholder conversation to understand context and decision pressure
  • Concise 2 to 3 page executive note with prioritised findings
  • Debrief session with clear next-step priorities
Outcome
A ranked, independent view of what is working, what is getting in the way, and what needs attention first. It helps the team decide where to focus before more budget, time, or trust is lost.
2 to 3 days · Delivered within 1 week
PEARL Focus Sprint
£8,000

Diagnosis turned into focused action. Over four weeks, the two most critical priorities are narrowed down, shaped properly, and revalidated so the team can stop guessing and move with confidence.

Teams rarely fail because they saw nothing. They fail because effort gets spread too thin, the loudest opinions drive the backlog, or engineering time goes into the wrong problems first. A full product review often surfaces 10 to 15 issues. The real challenge is deciding which two matter most, then making sure they are scoped, shaped, and validated properly before more time and money are committed.

What's included
  • Approximately 8 days of consultant engagement delivered over a 4 week period
  • Up to 2 dedicated days of in-product analysis to deepen understanding of the most critical risks
  • Prioritisation of the wider issue list down to the 2 highest-impact action items
  • Working sessions with the founder, CTO, and or product lead to shape the right response
  • Review of proposed changes before engineering time is committed
  • Revalidation in staging or pre-production to confirm the original risk has been reduced
  • Short practical summary of what was prioritised, what changed, and what to watch next
Outcome
Clear priorities, sharper judgement, and support shaping the right changes so effort is not spread across too many issues and engineering time is used where it will make the biggest difference.
4 week engagement
PEARL Readiness Programme
£15,000

A full product readiness engagement that shows where the product is most likely to fail in real use, what to fix first, and helps your team make the highest-risk changes properly.

A product rarely fails because nobody noticed the problems. It fails because risk is spread across the product, teams work from disconnected assumptions, and the fixes that matter most do not get shaped, implemented, and checked properly. When leadership is relying on a product to stand up to real users, investors, buyers, or partners, a partial review is not enough. They need a full picture of what is breaking across the product, what it is costing the business, and what has to change for the product to become safer to scale.

What's included
  • End-to-end PEARL review of the whole product, including the most critical workflows and points of user drop-off
  • Review of marketing, sales, and positioning materials against the actual in-product experience
  • Structured consultations with founders, senior leadership, product, engineering, and relevant user-facing teams
  • Analysis of available product data, including activation, usage, conversion, and retention signals
  • 5 to 10 prioritised findings mapped across Product, Engagement, Activation, Retention, and Leverage
  • Clear explanation of business impact, including lost trust, blocked growth, wasted spend, or avoidable operational risk
  • Concise executive report with a ranked action plan and references to specific screens, workflows, and pressure points
  • Strategic debrief with leadership to agree what needs attention now, next, and later
  • Hands-on support with the team to help shape the highest-priority fixes so they are reflected properly in the product
  • Retesting after changes in staging or early live use to verify that risk is genuinely coming down
  • Support planning how the updated product is put in front of users safely and credibly
Outcome
A product that is better prepared for real-world use, with the highest-risk issues identified, prioritised, addressed, and rechecked. Leadership gets a clearer view of what is true, the team gets a sharper order of attack, and the product is in a stronger position to scale without wasting more time, money, or trust.
One readiness cycle, scoped around your release rhythm
