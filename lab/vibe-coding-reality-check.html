Vibe code, but make it make sense.

A one-page reality check for the app you’re building right now — so you can see what is working, what needs simplifying, and what deserves a second pair of eyes.

You have already done something difficult: you turned an idea into something real. This is not a test of whether you are a “real” builder. It helps you decide whether your next move is build, test, simplify, or ask for help.

Think before you add

01 What does your app actually do?

Complete the sentence in the simplest way possible.

My app helps
so they can

First meaningful outcome (FMO)

What is working well?

Name three things you are proud of.

What feels fragile?

Note what feels confusing, unstable, or unfinished.

02 Map the activation path

List the exact steps a new user takes to reach the FMO.

Check: Is any step confusing? Does the order feel natural? Does value arrive quickly?

03 Feature reality check

Feature / idea Works reliably Works sometimes Not sure Needs rework

Look for the pattern: “Sometimes” and “Not sure” are useful signals, not failures.

04 How confident are you?

How confident are you that a new user can reach the FMO without confusion?

5
1 · not confident 10 · fully confident

That is a useful signal, not a verdict. A focused diagnostic can help you find the confusion before you put the app in front of more people.
